Write a LinkedIn post (under 1,300 characters) recapping last night's event for my feed.

Event: Integration Debt Night, hosted by Kestrelmoor (a developer-tools startup) at its Flatiron office. About 60 people came.

Panel:
- Priya Raman-Holt, VP Platform at Tallowbrook. Tallowbrook's website describes it as "the partner platform for mid-market logistics software".
- Marcus Feldane, founder of Quillary, which makes tooling that connects AI agents to a company's existing systems.

My notes:
- Tallowbrook maintains 140 partner integrations with a team of three engineers.
- Raman-Holt: "We stopped building connectors one by one and started publishing a spec. Partners build to it now."
- Feldane: agent pilots fail at the integration seam, not at the model. His team's rule is to wire up data access before choosing a model.
- Show of hands: about 40 of the 60 said an agent pilot at their company had stalled on getting data access.
- Disagreement: Raman-Holt thinks partners should own their connectors; Feldane thinks the platform has to own them or quality drifts.
