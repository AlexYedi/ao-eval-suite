Write a LinkedIn post (under 1,300 characters) recapping this morning's keynote.

Event: Fintech Builders Summit, opening keynote.

Speaker: Helena Voss-Marchetti, founder and CEO of Larchmont Systems (fraud-detection software for community banks). She founded and sold a payments company before this, has spent 20 years in payments infrastructure, and sits on two bank boards.

My notes from the keynote:
- Larchmont's customers are banks with under $5B in assets. Most have no data-science team.
- Her claim: small banks lose more to fraud per account than large banks because they buy rules, not models.
- Larchmont ships one shared model across 90 banks, trained on pooled, anonymised fraud labels.
- She expects regulators to require explainable fraud decisions within two years.
- Best line: "A rule you can't update by Friday is a rule the fraudsters already know."
